### D36. Unsupported or damaged stores need manual recovery of namespace changes

- **Why:** A full listing and backing bytes cannot reconstruct an unsent removal or a move
  whose meaning was held only in an unsupported or damaged layout.
- **Effect:** The database and its WAL, SHM and any older rollback journal are quarantined, readable pins and exceptions restored,
  and readable pending moves/removals exported with a count and path in LastError. They are
  never replayed automatically. The newest three quarantines are retained. A newer schema
  refuses to start; normal upgrades migrate sequentially after a WAL-inclusive snapshot.
- **Stays because:** Replaying guessed namespace changes could remove or move another device's
  work. A genuinely lost/unreadable database can only preserve the bytes that remain.
- **Where:** `crates/konedrive-tree/src/schema/old_store.rs`, `schema/quarantine.rs`,
  [store preservation](../design/store.md).
