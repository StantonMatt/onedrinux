### F66. Consent to write stays with Microsoft after the account turns read-only

- **Why:** Microsoft keeps the consent for the Microsoft account, and only its own consent page
  takes it back.
- **Effect:** Once an account allowed writing, its read-only refreshes are answered with
  `Files.ReadWrite` too (measured 2026-10-29), and the token very likely can write. A read-only
  account is kept from writing by KOneDrive alone: its folder refuses changes (`EROFS`), its
  sending is held, and the sender stops every step of an account `config.toml` sets read-only.
  We never hand the token out. The wider grant is expected for a configured read-only
  account and raises no error; `konedrivectl account mode read-only` names
  <https://account.live.com/consent/Manage>, where the user removes the permission.
- **Stays because:** Microsoft decides what a refresh returns; we cannot revoke consent from our
  side.
- **Where:** `crates/konedrived/src/account/state.rs` (`ModeNote::WiderGrant`),
  `crates/konedrived/src/onedrive_steps/write_scope.rs`
